Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IQScore Fun ("we", "us") handles information when you visit https://iqscore.fun.

Information we collect

Test answers. Your answers and your result are processed in your own browser. We do not send them to our servers and we do not store them.

Log and usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically record information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of the visit. We use this to keep the site running and to understand how it is used.

Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and message so we can reply.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site features.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (for example Google Analytics) to understand traffic. These services may collect information such as your approximate location and pages viewed.

Your rights (including California residents)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act). We do not knowingly sell personal information for money. To make a request, contact us at contact@iqscore.fun.

Children's privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
