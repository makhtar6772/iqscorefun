How the IQScore Fun practice test works

The test has 20 multiple-choice questions in three groups: 7 math puzzles (number sequences and word problems), 7 logical reasoning questions, and 6 visual pattern questions where you pick the figure that comes next. A 20-minute countdown timer runs during the test, and your answers are submitted automatically when it reaches 0:00. Most people finish in 10 to 15 minutes.

At the end you receive an estimated IQ score, a position on the IQ scale, and a question-by-question review so you can learn from the ones you missed.

Important: This is an entertainment and educational practice test. It is not a clinical IQ test and cannot diagnose or officially measure intelligence. See our disclaimer.
