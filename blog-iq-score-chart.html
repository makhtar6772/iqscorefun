IQ Score Chart: What Is a Good, Average or High IQ?

Most modern IQ tests are designed so the average score is 100, with a standard deviation of 15. Use the table below as a general guide. Labels vary a little between tests, and a score is always an estimate rather than an exact measurement.

IQ rangeCommon descriptionApprox. share of people
130 and aboveVery high / gifted rangeabout 2%
120 - 129Highabout 7%
110 - 119Above averageabout 16%
90 - 109Averageabout 50%
80 - 89Below averageabout 16%
70 - 79Borderline / lowabout 7%
Below 70Significantly below averageabout 2%

What is a good IQ score?

Any score between 90 and 110 is considered average, and about half of all people fall there. A score above 110 is above average, and a score of 130 or higher puts a person in roughly the top two percent. There is no "bad" IQ in terms of a person's worth. Scores describe performance on a specific kind of test, not a person's value.

Famous myths about high IQ

  • High IQ does not guarantee success or happiness.
  • Online tests that promise a "genius" result to everyone are not reliable.
  • IQ is not the same as knowledge. Knowledge can be learned at any age.
