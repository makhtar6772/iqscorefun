About IQScore Fun

IQScore Fun is a free website that lets anyone take a short IQ practice test and learn how intelligence scores work. We built it because many online IQ tests hide the result behind paywalls or ask for personal details. Ours does neither.

Our mission

To make reasoning puzzles and clear, honest information about IQ available to everyone, for free.

How we make money

The site is supported by advertising. Ads allow us to keep the test free and to keep writing new articles and puzzles.

Honest about what we are

Our test is for entertainment and education. It is not a clinical or professionally standardized assessment, and we do not claim otherwise. Read our disclaimer.
